# Wing Response Matrix - 9 Wings x 5 Phases

Mandate-driven expected actions for the required NDMA wings. The source of truth for application behavior is `data/Mandate/mandate.json`.

## 1. Technical Early Warning Wing (Tech EW)

| Phase | Key Actions |
|---|---|
| **D Day** | Validate the hazard trigger; issue the first impact-based warning; publish initial hazard, exposure, and risk maps; brief response wings on secondary risks |
| **D+1 to D+5** | Maintain real-time hazard monitoring; refresh dashboards and maps; identify hotspots and cascading risks; provide NEOC technical analytics |
| **D+5 to D+10** | Support damage, loss, and needs assessment; assess warning performance and data gaps; refine hazard layers; document technical lessons |
| **D+10 to D+20** | Produce risk-informed recovery analytics; update hazard databases; coordinate technical recovery inputs; prepare post-event findings |
| **D+20 to D+50** | Finalize post-event risk analysis; recommend monitoring and dashboard upgrades; archive validated datasets; support future SimEx design |

## 2. National Institute of Disaster Management (NIDM)

| Phase | Key Actions |
|---|---|
| **D Day** | Activate observer, volunteer, and training channels; identify HR support gaps; provide evidence-based inputs; start live documentation |
| **D+1 to D+5** | Track trained volunteer deployments; coordinate technical HR; engage academic rapid assessment partners; capture relief and learning lessons |
| **D+5 to D+10** | Consolidate field feedback; identify training gaps; support impact reviews; issue guidance to academic and volunteer networks |
| **D+10 to D+20** | Prepare recovery capacity-building inputs; document transition challenges; recommend training modules; feed lessons into improvement plans |
| **D+20 to D+50** | Finalize after-action learning; update disaster archives; integrate findings into curricula; publish implementation guidance |

## 3. Technical Equipment & Maintenance Wing (Tech E & M)

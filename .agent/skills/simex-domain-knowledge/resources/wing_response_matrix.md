# Wing Response Matrix — 10 Wings × 5 Phases

Complete matrix of expected actions for each NDMA wing across all disaster response phases for the Batagram KP M7.4 earthquake scenario.

---

## 1. Tech Early Warning

| Phase | Key Actions |
|---|---|
| **D Day** | Issue earthquake alert via NEOC; activate seismic monitoring stations; coordinate with PMD for aftershock forecasting; disseminate public safety advisories |
| **D+1 to D+5** | Monitor aftershock sequence; issue aftershock warnings; provide seismic analysis briefs to Ops Wing; liaise with international seismological agencies |
| **D+5 to D+10** | Continue aftershock monitoring; assess secondary hazard risks (landslides, dam stability); update hazard maps; provide daily seismic briefs |
| **D+10 to D+20** | Transition to reduced monitoring; publish aftershock trend analysis; support DRR with updated seismic hazard assessment for reconstruction |
| **D+20 to D+50** | Produce post-earthquake seismic analysis report; update national seismic hazard maps; recommend early warning system upgrades |

## 2. Ops Wing

| Phase | Key Actions |
|---|---|
| **D Day** | Activate NEOC to 24/7 operations; issue first SitRep within 2 hours; coordinate initial search & rescue (SAR) deployment; establish communication with PDMA KP and DDMA Batagram |
| **D+1 to D+5** | Coordinate multi-agency SAR operations; produce daily SitReps; manage resource allocation from national stockpiles; coordinate military deployment for SAR |
| **D+5 to D+10** | Transition from SAR to relief operations; manage camp coordination; produce consolidated needs assessment; coordinate international assistance arrivals |
| **D+10 to D+20** | Shift focus to early recovery coordination; manage inter-wing resource conflicts; facilitate transition from relief to recovery; produce weekly operational summaries |
| **D+20 to D+50** | Oversee recovery operations coordination; manage phase-down of emergency operations; compile comprehensive operations report; coordinate lessons learned workshop |

## 3. Logistics

| Phase | Key Actions |
|---|---|
| **D Day** | Activate emergency supply chain; dispatch initial relief goods (tents, blankets, food) from nearest warehouse; assess road access to Batagram |
| **D+1 to D+5** | Establish forward logistics base; coordinate air drops for inaccessible areas; manage warehouse inventory; coordinate fuel supplies for generators and vehicles |
| **D+5 to D+10** | Scale up supply distribution; establish distribution points in affected areas; manage cold chain for medical supplies; coordinate winterized shelter materials |
| **D+10 to D+20** | Transition to sustained supply operations; optimize distribution networks; manage return of borrowed military transport assets; begin procurement for recovery phase |
| **D+20 to D+50** | Support reconstruction material supply chain; wind down emergency logistics; inventory remaining relief stocks; produce logistics performance report |

## 4. Disaster Risk Reduction (DRR)

| Phase | Key Actions |
|---|---|
| **D Day** | Activate rapid damage assessment teams; issue community safety advisories; assess immediate secondary hazard risks (landslides, structural collapse) |
| **D+1 to D+5** | Deploy detailed damage assessment teams; coordinate with communities on evacuation needs; identify high-risk zones for aftershock-triggered landslides |
| **D+5 to D+10** | Compile comprehensive vulnerability assessment; issue building safety advisories; support camp site selection (avoiding hazard zones); community awareness on aftershock safety |
| **D+10 to D+20** | Develop risk-informed recovery framework; produce hazard maps for reconstruction planning; design community resilience programs; train community disaster committees |
| **D+20 to D+50** | Lead Build Back Better strategy; update district risk profile; develop multi-hazard risk reduction plan; community-based DRM training programs |

## 5. Gender and Child Cell (GCC)

| Phase | Key Actions |
|---|---|
| **D Day** | Issue protection alerts; activate GBV helplines; assess immediate protection needs for women and children |
| **D+1 to D+5** | Establish women/child-friendly spaces in camps; deploy protection monitoring teams; coordinate child tracing and reunification; distribute dignity kits |
| **D+5 to D+10** | Scale up psychosocial support services; monitor GBV incidents; ensure gender-disaggregated data in assessments; coordinate with UNICEF and UN Women |
| **D+10 to D+20** | Establish sustained protection programs; support school resumption planning; women's livelihood recovery programs; child protection case management |
| **D+20 to D+50** | Long-term psychosocial recovery programs; advocate for gender-responsive reconstruction; child education restoration; protection mainstreaming in recovery |

## 6. Provincial Coordination Cell (PCC)

| Phase | Key Actions |
|---|---|
| **D Day** | Activate communication with PDMA KP and DDMA Batagram; coordinate provincial emergency declarations; assess provincial resource availability |
| **D+1 to D+5** | Facilitate federal-provincial resource transfer; coordinate PDMA relief operations; manage multi-province coordination if spillover areas affected |
| **D+5 to D+10** | Coordinate provincial capacity gaps with federal resources; facilitate international NGO access to affected areas; manage provincial political coordination |
| **D+10 to D+20** | Support provincial recovery planning; coordinate compensation disbursement; facilitate provincial reconstruction taskforce formation |
| **D+20 to D+50** | Oversee provincial recovery progress; coordinate long-term federal-provincial reconstruction support; facilitate provincial lessons learned |

## 7. Tech E & M (Equipment & Machinery)

| Phase | Key Actions |
|---|---|
| **D Day** | Deploy heavy rescue equipment to Batagram; mobilize engineering units; assess road clearing requirements |
| **D+1 to D+5** | Operate heavy machinery for SAR (cranes, excavators); clear blocked roads (KKH and access roads); provide emergency generators for hospitals and NEOC |
| **D+5 to D+10** | Continue road clearance operations; establish temporary river crossings for damaged bridges; provide engineering support for camp setup; restore critical water supply infrastructure |
| **D+10 to D+20** | Repair critical infrastructure (bridges, roads); support temporary school and clinic construction; maintain emergency power systems |
| **D+20 to D+50** | Support reconstruction engineering assessments; return/maintain heavy equipment; produce engineering operations report; recommend equipment procurement for future preparedness |

## 8. Regional Military and Media Wing

| Phase | Key Actions |
|---|---|
| **D Day** | Coordinate military helicopter deployment for aerial assessment; issue first media brief; activate military SAR teams; establish media coordination cell |
| **D+1 to D+5** | Manage military air bridge for inaccessible areas; daily media briefings; coordinate with ISPR; manage social media monitoring and counter-misinformation |
| **D+5 to D+10** | Coordinate military logistics support; manage media access to affected areas; produce human interest stories for public awareness; coordinate VIP visits |
| **D+10 to D+20** | Plan military phase-down; transition media narrative to recovery; coordinate donor visibility events; manage public expectations on reconstruction timeline |
| **D+20 to D+50** | Complete military withdrawal plan; final media campaign on recovery progress; coordinate with media for anniversary/memorial planning; produce communication report |

## 9. National Institute of Disaster Management (NIDM)

| Phase | Key Actions |
|---|---|
| **D Day** | Deploy observation teams to document response in real-time; activate training rosters for surge deployment |
| **D+1 to D+5** | Document response effectiveness; provide on-ground training support for newly deployed volunteers; compile real-time lessons |
| **D+5 to D+10** | Conduct interim response assessment; support camp management training; document coordination challenges and best practices |
| **D+10 to D+20** | Design recovery phase training program; produce interim lessons learned report; plan community disaster preparedness training for affected areas |
| **D+20 to D+50** | Produce comprehensive lessons learned report; design updated training curriculum; recommend policy improvements; plan next simulation exercise based on gaps identified |

## 10. Infra Audit and Project Development Wing

| Phase | Key Actions |
|---|---|
| **D Day** | Activate rapid infrastructure damage assessment teams; coordinate with seismic engineers for building safety checks |
| **D+1 to D+5** | Deploy structural engineers for detailed damage assessment; tag buildings (red/yellow/green safety ratings); assess critical infrastructure (hospitals, schools, government buildings) |
| **D+5 to D+10** | Compile comprehensive infrastructure damage report; estimate reconstruction costs; identify priority infrastructure for emergency repair |
| **D+10 to D+20** | Develop reconstruction project proposals; coordinate with international donors on infrastructure funding; design Build Back Better specifications for earthquake-resistant construction |
| **D+20 to D+50** | Finalize reconstruction master plan; manage project development pipeline; coordinate with provincial reconstruction authority; produce infrastructure audit report for government |

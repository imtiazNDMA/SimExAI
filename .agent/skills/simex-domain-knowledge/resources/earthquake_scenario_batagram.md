## Geographic & Seismic Context

- **Batagram** is a mountainous district in Mansehra Division, KP, near the 2005 earthquake epicenter
- Located on the **Hazara-Kashmir Syntaxis**, one of the most seismically active zones in South Asia
- **Dhurnal Fault** and **Gabbar Fault** run through the region
- Terrain: Steep valleys, limited road access, altitude 600–4,500m
- The 2005 M7.6 earthquake killed ~73,000 people in this region

| **Vulnerable Groups** | High proportion of children, elderly in remote areas |

## Expected Impact (M7.4 Scenario)

### Casualties & Displacement
- **Estimated fatalities**: 2,000–5,000
- **Estimated injuries**: 10,000–25,000
- **Displaced population**: 80,000–150,000
- **Houses damaged/destroyed**: 30,000–60,000

### Infrastructure Damage
- **Roads**: Major landslides blocking KKH and access roads
- **Bridges**: Multiple bridge collapses in valleys
- **Hospitals**: District HQ hospital partially collapsed; rural health units destroyed
- **Schools**: Widespread school building collapses
- **Power**: Grid failure across district, estimated 7–14 day restoration
- **Telecom**: Cell towers damaged, partial communication blackout
- **Water**: Pipeline damage, contamination risk from landslides

### Secondary Hazards
- **Aftershocks**: M5.0+ aftershocks expected for weeks
- **Landslides**: Ongoing risk due to unstable terrain
- **Winter exposure**: Hypothermia risk for displaced populations without shelter
- **Disease outbreak**: Risk of waterborne diseases (cholera, dysentery)
- **Dam/reservoir risk**: Tarbela Dam proximity requires monitoring

## Key Coordination Challenges

1. **Access**: Mountain roads blocked by landslides — air support essential
2. **Communication**: Telecom disruption delays damage assessment
3. **Weather**: Winter cold compounds shelter emergency
4. **Scale**: 400K population with limited local capacity
5. **Multi-agency**: Military, PDMA KP, DDMA Batagram, UN agencies, NGOs all activated
6. **Aftershock management**: Secondary collapses during SAR operations
